\begin{multicols}{2}
Intractorticical microelectrode arrays are widely used in motor brain-machine interfaces (BMIs), 
by recording spike activities as input signals \citep{stavisky2015high}. 
Spikes are a group of neural action potentials, and can be discriminated as single neuron activity by their waveforms, 
since different neurons fire with different patterns. 
The brain activity data with single-neuron resolution provided remarkable magnitude and decoding precision \citep{lazarevich2018neural}.
These extracellular recordings have enabled non-human primates to accurately control computer cursors \citep{serruya2002instant, makin2018}, illustrated in Figure \ref{fig:intro_monkey},
or robotic limbs \citep{carmena2003learning} or its own musulature \citep{ethier2012restoration}. 
Except from motor cortex signals, a research about the self-location ability of rodents, 
also used spike signals from the place cells and pyramidal neurons found in mammalian hippocampus, 
to reconstruct the trajectory of the rodents in a $ 100cm \times 100cm $ square \citep{tampuu2019efficient}.

\section{Difference between center-out task and pursuit task with point-to-point trajectory}
\label{sec:difference_between_center_out_and_pursuit}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\todo[inline]{center-out task and pursuit task的差異，Pursuit task with point-to-point trajectory，可參考以下論文，搜尋An Improved Unscented Kalman Filter Based Decoder for Cortical Brain-Machine Interfaces}
\cite{li2016improved} is one of a few researches that has conducted both two kinds of movement.
\begin{itemize}
    \item Center-out task requires subjects to move the cursor from center target to peripheral targets, normally located at a fix radial distance between the center.
    \item Center-out task restricts the variety of movement directions, researches typically use 8 outer targets.
    \item The movement from pursuit task with point-to-point trajectory is more complex than center-out task, since there are more combinations to select two random targets in a 2D plane.
    \item The cursor in pursuit task moves continuously by the subject, there is no need of returning back to origin after completing a target reach.
    \item Finding two similar movements from two randomly selected targets in pursuit tasks is very unlikely to happen, whereas in center-out task it is rather easy.
\end{itemize}
\end{multicols}

\section{Inspirations and goals of our research}
\begin{multicols}{2}

Since neural signals are non-linear sequential data \citep{brezina2000neuromuscular}, and firing pattern in motor cortex neurons have a lot to do with the movement direction \citep{hatsopoulos1998information},
It is practical to use RNNs as the backbone of neural decoders \citep{buonomano2011population}, for RNN has outperformed traditional linear decoder \citep{sussillo2016making}.
New technique has been created for RNNs \citep{ba2016layer}, it gives us chance to boost decoding accuracy.
However, previous BCI researches involved RNNs have not focus on the internal dynamics of their models and explain how the model actually learn from the neural signal.
Inspired by the developement of attention module in natural language processing, 
we believe it can provide potent reasons for us to explain our the design of our model and brand new insight to neural science by showing the attention map of neural signals.
\end{multicols}

\section{Motivation and Objective}
\label{sec: motivation}

\begin{multicols}{2}
This research is aimed for developing algorithms that can convert neural signals into actual movement of the limbs. 
For those who suffer from motor disability, there exist softwares that allow people to move or control a computer cursor using imagined or attempted movents \citep{hosman2019bci}, 
or driving an exoskeleton to perform motro-related task for stroke rehabitation, as \cite{chowdhury2018active} successfully developed such EEG-based BCI supported neurorehabilitation system. 
By implementing the advanced techniques of deep learning and exerting all possible computational resources, we stand great chance of developing an algorithm that outperforms current studies. 
\end{multicols}
