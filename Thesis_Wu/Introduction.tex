\begin{multicols}{2}
    Intractorticical microelectrode arrays are widely used in motor brain-machine interfaces (BMIs), 
    by recording spike activities as input signals \citep{stavisky2015high}. 
    Spikes are a group of neural action potentials, and can be discriminated as single neuron activity, 
    since different neurons fire with different patterns. 
    These extracellular recordings have enabled non-human primates to accurately control computer cursors \citep{serruya2002instant, makin2018}, illustrated in Figure \ref{fig:intro_monkey},
    or robotic limbs \citep{carmena2003learning} or its own musulature \citep{ethier2012restoration}. 
    Except from motor cortex signals, a research about the self-location ability of rodents, 
    also used spike signals from the place cells and pyramidal neurons found in mammalian hippocampus, 
    to reconstruct the trajectory of the rodents in a $ 100cm \times 100cm $ square \citep{tampuu2019efficient}.
    \end{multicols}

\section{Motivation and Objective}
\label{sec: motivation}

\begin{multicols}{2}
This research is aimed for developing algorithms that can convert neural signals into actual movement of the limbs. 
For those who suffer from motor disability, there exist softwares that allow people to move or control a computer cursor using imagined or attempted movents \citep{hosman2019bci}, 
or driving an exoskeleton to perform motro-related task for stroke rehabitation, as \cite{chowdhury2018active} successfully developed such EEG-based BCI supported neurorehabilitation system. 
By implementing the advanced techniques of deep learning and exerting all possible computational resources, we stand great chance of developing an algorithm that outperforms current studies. 
\end{multicols}

\section{Delimitation}
\label{sec:delimitation}

\begin{multicols}{2}
In order to reduce the difficulty of data acquisition, we only consider neural signal from monkeys instead of human or other kinds of animal. 
Furthermore, no animal experiments were conducted in this reseach. All of hand movement decoding were computed off-line.
\end{multicols}
