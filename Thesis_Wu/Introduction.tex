\section{Why, what and how is invasive BCI?}
\label{sec:why_what_how_is_invasive_BCI}
% \begin{multicols}{2}
\todo[inline, color=green!40]{為什麼需要侵入式腦機介面、侵入式腦機介面的用途?}
\todo[inline, author=Prof.]{ \ref{sec:why_what_how_is_invasive_BCI}不是講electrode的用途，而是 BCI 的用途，要幫助哪些人?}
\todo[inline, author=Wu, color=blue!40]{ BCI 的用途改放在第一段 }
This research is aimed for developing algorithms that can convert neural signals into actual movement of the limbs. 
For those who suffer from motor disability, 
there exist softwares that allow people to move or control a computer cursor using imagined or attempted movents \citep{hosman2019bci}, 
or driving an exoskeleton to perform motro-related task for stroke rehabitation, 
as \cite{chowdhury2018active} successfully developed such EEG-based brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) supported neurorehabilitation system. 
By implementing the advanced techniques of deep learning and exerting all possible computational resources, 
we stand great chance of developing an algorithm that outperforms current studies. 
\\

Intractorticical microelectrode arrays are widely used in motor BCIs,
by recording spike activities as input signals \citep{stavisky2015high}. 
Spikes are a group of neural action potentials, 
and can be discriminated as single neuron activity by their waveforms, 
since different neurons fire with different patterns. 
The brain activity data with single-neuron resolution provided remarkable magnitude and decoding precision \citep{lazarevich2018neural}.
These extracellular recordings have enabled non-human primates to accurately control computer cursors \citep{serruya2002instant, makin2018}, 
as we illustrated in Figure \ref{fig:intro_monkey}.
Also it allows patients to control the robotic limbs \citep{carmena2003learning} or its own musulature \citep{ethier2012restoration}. 
Except from motor cortex signals, 
a research about the self-location ability of rodents, 
adopted spike signals from the place cells and pyramidal neurons found in mammalian hippocampus to reconstruct the trajectory of the rodents in a $ 100cm \times 100cm $ square \citep{tampuu2019efficient}.

\section{Difference between center-out task and pursuit task with point-to-point trajectory}
\label{sec:difference_between_center_out_and_pursuit}
% \begin{multicols}{2}

\todo[inline, color=green!40]{center-out task and pursuit task的差異}
\todo[inline, author=Prof.]{Pursuit task with point-to-point trajectory，可參考以下論文，搜尋An Improved Unscented Kalman Filter Based Decoder for Cortical Brain-Machine Interfaces}
\cite{li2016improved} is one of a few researches that has conducted both kinds of movements.
Based on the its descriptions we can list the differences between center-out and point-to-point task with the following points:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Center-out task requires subjects to move the cursor from center target to peripheral targets, normally located at a fix radial distance between the center.
    \item Center-out task restricts the variety of movement directions, researches typically use 8 outer targets.
    \item The movement from pursuit task with point-to-point trajectory is more complex than center-out task, since there are more combinations to select two random targets in a 2D plane.
    \item The cursor in pursuit task moves continuously by the subject, there is no need to return the cursor back to the origin after completing a target reach.
    \item Finding two similar movements from two randomly selected targets in pursuit tasks is very unlikely to happen, whereas in center-out task it is rather easy.
\end{itemize}

\todo[inline, author=Wu, color=blue!40]{現有方法 Kalman filter 的相關問題}
The standard Kalman filter uses linear functions to map the kinematics between current and the next steps, and it also assume Gaussian distributions in the spike count data.
Several non-linear tuning methods were developed and used in BCI such as Unscented Kalman filter \citep{li2016improved},
but it failed to learn a useful latent representation \citep{makin2018}.
A new non-linear tuning method \textit{Recurrent exponential-family harmonium} is proposed by \cite{makin2018},
which they claimed that it outperforms Unscented Kalman filter in neural decoding. 
\\

\todo[ inline, author=Prof]{說明 \cite{makin2018} 哪裡還不夠準?}
\todo[ inline, author=Wu, color=blue!40]{\cite{makin2018} 的 acceleration 預測相當差，但是只能從他們給的$R^2$來說明，無法做他們詳細的的 Failure case study}
Although they have obtained high performances in the position and velocity decoding tasks, 
but failed to decode acceleration with the accuracy as good as the preceding two kinematic variables.
Other improved methods for Kalman filter in \cite{vaskov2018cortical} requires two stage of training,
and it must acquire the target coordinates in the second training phase.
\\

\todo[inline, author=Wu, color=blue!40]{詳細說明這些RNN方法改良了甚麼問題}
RNNs are the most promising tool to outperform Kalman filter in BCI \cite{hosman2019bci}.
Although RNNs are expensive in space and time complexities, 
but they are not restricted to solving only linear system with Gaussian noise assumption \citep{chenna2004state}.
\\

\todo[inline, author=Wu, color=blue!40]{這一段引用其他有使用sequence model的研究}
\todo[inline, author=Wu, color=blue!40]{說明 RNN 為何可以解 neural decoding}
Since neural signals are non-linear sequential data \citep{brezina2000neuromuscular}, 
and firing pattern in motor cortex neurons have a lot to do with the movement direction \citep{hatsopoulos1998information},
It is practical to use RNNs as the backbone of neural decoders \citep{buonomano2011population}, 
for RNN has outperformed traditional linear decoder \citep{sussillo2016making}.
New technique has been created for RNNs \citep{ba2016layer}, 
it gives us chance to boost decoding accuracy.
However, previous BCI researches involved RNNs have not focus on the internal dynamics of their models and explain how the model actually learn from the neural signal.
Deep learn models are criticized as black boxes with its lack of interpretability.
\\

\todo[inline, author=Wu, color=blue!40]{這段提到使用 neural decoder 一定會遇到 tap size 選擇的問題}
Most of the BCI decoders choosed the length of spiking history arbitrarity and they adjusted empirically based on the decoding performances \citep{tseng2019decoding},
as in \cite{li2009unscented, li2016improved} used 10 taps of spiking data mainly in their experiments.
There is no analytical tool for researchers to evaluate the optimal history length yet,
and we tried to solve this problem with our visualization tool.

\section{Inspirations and goals of our research}
\label{sec:inspirations_and_goals}
% \begin{multicols}{2}
\todo[inline, color=green!40]{碩士論文受到哪些論文的啟發? 有了甚麼想法? 想解決哪些問題?}
\todo[inline, author=Prof.]{NLP的attention有甚麼特別的地方?NLP的問題有甚麼特性?neural decoding的問題又有甚麼特性?這兩者之間有甚麼關聯?}
\todo[inline, author=Wu, color=blue!40]{新增NLP與Neural decoder的關聯}
The resemblances between NLP neural network models and neural signal decoders are strong.
First, since they are poth sequential data and rely on extracting temporal features \citep{pustejovsky2005temporal, kornysheva2014human},
that makes RNNs widely used in both fields \citep{yin2017comparative, glaser2020machine, xu2019comparison}.
Second, various types of NLP tasks exist and they have inconsistant input-output types,
such as machine translation has multiple inputs multiple outputs \citep{bahdanau2014neural, luong2015effective, vaswani2017attention},
and sentiment analysis or classification with multiple inputs single outputs \citep{cheng2016long, yang2016hierarchical, wang2016attention}.
Corresponding to our neural decoding task, models with multiple inputs single outputs are most applicable in our case.
\\

\todo[inline, author=Wu, color=blue!40]{使用NLP attention的理由。}
This research is inspired by the successful work from the attention module who enhanced the RNN performance and also provided interpretability in natural language processing \citep{lin2017structured}.
\todo[inline, author=Prof]{說明NLP attention的用途}
After implementing the attention module in the setiment classification tasks, 
the prediction accuracy rised accourding to their experiments.
Also in the field of image processing,
attention modules have provided visualization to explain what the model sees on their input images \citep{hu2018squeeze, woo2018cbam}.
\todo[inline, author=Prof]{說明neural decoding 需 attention 的理由}
But currently there are no other researches showing such visualization results about neural signals.
As the resemblances bewteen NLP and neural signals were mentioned earlier in this section,
we believe it can provide potent reasons for us to explain our the design of our model and brand new insight to neural science by showing the attention map of neural signals.
\\

\todo[inline, author=Wu, color=blue!40]{提出為什麼需要analysis of reciprocal effect}
So far no other reseaches have conducted experiments on analyzing which technique contibutes more on the neural decoding. 
We are interested how the the normalization method \citep{ba2016layer} and the attention module \citep{lin2017structured} can effect each other's performances.
