\section{Motivation and Objective}
\label{sec: motivation}
This research is aimed for developing algorithms that can convert neural signals into actual movement of the limbs. 
For those who suffer from motor disability, there exist softwares that allow people to move or control a computer cursor using imagined or attempted movents \citep{hosman2019bci}, 
or driving an exoskeleton to perform motro-related task for stroke rehabitation, as \cite{chowdhury2018active} successfully developed such EEG-based BCI supported neurorehabilitation system. 
By implementing the advanced techniques of deep learning and exerting all possible computational resources, we stand great chance of developing an algorithm that outperforms current studies. 


\section{Delimitation}
\label{sec:delimitation}
In order to reduce the difficulty of data acquisition, we only consider neural signal from monkeys instead of human or other kinds of animal. 
Furthermore, no animal experiments were conducted in this reseach. All of hand movement decoding were computed off-line.

by recording spike activities as input signals \citep{stavisky2015high}. 
Spikes are a group of neural action potentials, and can be discriminated as single neuron activity, 
since different neurons fire with different patterns. 
These extracellular recordings have enabled non-human primates to accurately control computer cursors \citep{serruya2002instant, makin2018}, illustrated in Figure \ref{fig:intro_monkey},
or robotic limbs \citep{carmena2003learning} or its own musulature \citep{ethier2012restoration}. 
Except from motor cortex signals, a research about the self-location ability of rodents, 
also used spike signals from the place cells and pyramidal neurons found in mammalian hippocampus, 
to reconstruct the trajectory of the rodents in a $ 100cm \times 100cm $ square \citep{tampuu2019efficient}.

However, the spike recordings are not stable and the number of recorded single-units neurons may diminish over time \citep{ahmadi2019end}.

And also local motor potentials (LMPs), which are the moving average of LFPs. 
Due to the considerably low frequency of LFPs, they can be sampled, processed, and transmitted with lower power consuption \citep{flint2012accurate}.

\section{Recurrent Neural Networks}
\label{sec:rnn}
The first idea of recurrent neural networks (RNN), which is called the Hopfield networks, was introduced in 1980s \citep{hopfield1982neural}, 
and the following different versions of RNN have been developed afterwards \citep{elman1990finding, jordan1997serial}, as showed in Figure~\ref{fig:elman_and_jordan}. 
But the these RNN models face the same gradient vanishing problem, and the two effective approaches to solve this problem is either clip and scale the gradient if the norm is too large, 
or create a better RNN model \citep{alom2018history}.

Therefore, \cite{hochreiter1997long} created a well-known model named Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM), solved the gradient vanishing problem of the original RNNs. LSTM quipped with three gates to learn how to forget or keep the memory from previous states. 
Followed by the work of \cite{chung2014empirical}, a new model named Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) which is similar to LSTM, was introduced. 
It is has gained popularity in the community due to its efficiency in training and the high performance sometimes even outdoes the LSTM. 

Nowadays, RNNs come with various architectures have benn widely used in BMI reseachers \citep{ahmadi2020impact}, \cite{tuleuov2019deep} even developed a hybrid deep leaning model which combined RNN with convolution neural network (CNN). 
These preceding works manifest the uprising potentials of RNNs in computational neural science.

\section{Attention Mechanism}
\label{sec:atten}
\cite{sutskever2014sequence} is the first research which implemented the encoder-decoder machanism (or called sequence to sequence, seq2seq) to machine translation realm.
This structure has gained popularity also in sentiment analysis ever since.
The structure of encoder-decoder RNN illustrated in Figure~\ref{fig:rnn_encoder_decoder}, 
where the encoder turns a source sequence into a contex vector, then the decoder will use contex vector as the first hidden state to start translation.
In the traning phase, decoder will be provided the actual target sequence wheras in testing phase, 
the output from previous timestep will be the input of current timestep.

\begin{figure}[H]
    \begin{center}
        \includegraphics[width=1\textwidth]{rnn_encoder_decoder.png}
        \caption{RNN encoder-decoder}
        \label{fig:rnn_encoder_decoder}
    \end{center}
\end{figure}

But when it comes to processing long time-sequence data, inevitably will the models facing the problem called long-term dependencies. 
This is primarily due to the fact that the longer the sequence lenght, 
the higher the possibility of gradient vanishing might occur in RNNs \citep{chandar2019towards}. 
Especially in neural machine translation researches, where long input sentences are apt to cause translation errors.
Thus a novel architecture for neural machine translation customed to machine translation was invented, named \textit{alignment model}
It was proposed in \cite{bahdanau2014neural}, which they put an additional weights on the original outputs of an RNN. 
The alighment model scores how well the inputs around one position matches the output on another position, 
such that model is capable of focusing on the information relevant part of the input sentence, 
and leave a major positive impact on the ability of the neural machine translation system. 
Furthermore, by pointing out the parts where the model cosider informative allows humans to have more insights into the decision factors inside deep learning, since deep learning methods have been criticized as black box oracles for a long time \citep{brown2018recurrent, hudson2018compositional}. 

Apart form alignment model, \textit{self-attention} of \textit{intra-attention} works quiet differently.
The alighment model calculate the relationship between source and target sequences, 
while self-attention only consider relationship beteen the elements from the same input sequence.
It was \cite{cheng2016long} which first proposed the \textit{intra-attention} mechanism, and followed by the work of \cite{parikh2016decomposable,paulus2017deep}.
Then \cite{vaswani2017attention} adapted similar idea but discarded the RNN architecture, making its \textit{Transformer} model has the advantage of parallelization.

Similar attention techniques can be seen in sentiment classification research \citep{wang2016attention}, 
where they appended the attention mechanism right after the LSTM outputs and named it attention-based LSTM.
They also visualized the aspect in each sentence, e.g., the term "service" or "food" by showing the weights in attention vectors.
They can tell the important words from the whole sentence.

Several different means of attention mechanism were also implemented in BCI field.
\cite{zhang2020motor} used both CNN and RNN to extract the spatio and temporal dynamics in EEG signal processing, 
and its mechanism named \textit{Convolutional Recurrent Attention Model}.
The self-attention module was placed in the last layer, after the raw signal was first processed by CNN and then RNN.
The proposed method, convolutional recurrent attention model outperformed other state-of-the-art methods in EEG classification tasks.



\section{Normalization}
\label{sec:norm}
When training a deep learning model, \textit{internal covariate shift} as the change in distribution of network activations, is an issue that hinders the loss of the model to converge and sometimes incurs poor performances. 
Several novel approaches related to data normalization were designed to cope with this problem, showed in Table \ref{tab:six_types_of_norm}. 
Most of the measures calculate the means and variances from layer inputs. 
But due to the design, the effect of a normalization measure might vary in different deep learning models, e.g. batch normalization is not suitable for RNNs. 
Also in the field of image processing, generative adversarial network (GAN) thrives as well as its own normalization methods.
