\section{Connections between our AI model and neuroscience}
% \begin{multicols}{2}
\todo[inline, color=green!40]{探討網路內部的運作方式跟大腦的關連}

Both fields of AI and neuroscience has had a huge progress in recent years \cite{hassabis2017neuroscience}.
The relationship between these two fields has mainly two parts.
Neuroscience is a sources of inspiration for new types of algorithms or architectures in AI,
which is both complementary and independent to the mathematical and logic-based methods.
Neuroscience can also provide validation of the existing AI techniques,
and being a support for the plausiblily.
Biological plausiblily can act as a guide, but it's not a strict requirement in engineering.
\\

Take the image attention for example, 
The CNN model works on entire images, with equal priority given to all the pixels in the image.
However, this is not how the primate visual system actual works.
Instead of processing all input in parallel, 
visual attention shifts among different locations or objects in the image with strategies.
It is capable of centering and processing resources on a series in turn.
Neurocomputational models have show this piecemeal approach is benefitial,
that at any given moment, it isolating and prioritizing relevant information.
\\

In our study, the attention map showed that the decoder can capture two states of fingertips: standstill or in action.
Especially in position decoder, 
the decoder will predict the stationary trajectories based on the last moment when fingertips were still moving.
This result is match by the discovery in \cite{kaufman2016largest},
as they claimed that the neuron activities carry mostly the information about \textit{when} the movement will occur,
but do not carry essential information about \textit{what} movement it will be.

The reasons why implementing temporal attention did not contribute to decoding accuracy can be considered in two parts.
First of all, the temporal feature of neural spiking data we used can be extracted simply by an ordinary RNN.
With the aid of layer normalization to regulate the internal distribution,
our decoder can already reach the performances higher than the state-of-the-art results without attention module.
\\

Secondly, as our attention map has demonstrated that only a few time bins of firing rate are needed in decoding velocity and acceleration.
It contradicted the purpose of temporal attention, 
which is invented to solve the long-term dependency problems happened in decoding long sequences.

\cite{goodman2019postural} has presented that neurons in M1 are more related to velocity, rather than the time-varying posture.
\cite{okorokova2020decoding} has found out that neurons in motor cortex are more prefereable to encode velocity than its position. 
This is the main reason why velocity decoders outperform the position decoders.
\cite{kim2008neural,dethier2013design} showed that the coorelation between firing rate and velocity is significantly higher than the correlation between firing rate and position, 
in both random pursuit-tracking task and center-out-back task. 
These results suggested when developing the system for tetraplegia patients,
the decoding method should be based on the cursor velocity instead of the cursor position.
To compare the length of the past signals been used to decode the current kinematics in other researches, 
and justify that it is legit to use 20 time bins (1280ms) in decoding position, 
and 10 time bins (640ms) to decode velocity and acceleration,
we noticed that \cite{wu2003neural} has analysed the optimal length, 
which is between 500ms to 1500ms.
Which means our usage of the time bins is acceptable and reasonable.
