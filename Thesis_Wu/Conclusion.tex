We have successsfully implemented RNN and other state-of-the-art techniques to reach higher decoding performances than previous studies.
All three kinds of kinematic variables, position, velocity and acceleration improved with their $R^2$.
Compared to \cite{makin2018}, the acceleration improved the most,
and the kinematics of x-axis increased more than the ones of y-axis.
\\

Our research offered a novel way of neural information visualization.
With the aid of temporal attention module,
it is easier for human to gain insight into how does the RNN learn from the input neural signals,
along with the fingertip trajectories, which are the output.
Our attention map showed an obvious pattern which is strongly related to the fingertip's movement,
and no other BCI researches have provided.
\\

The interpretations our attention map provided are
\begin{itemize}
    \item Reasons for choosing a specific number as the length of spiking history 
    \item Why bidirectional RNN surpasses one-way RNN in neural decoding
    \item Why splitting position, velocity and acceleration into 3 individual decoders
    \item Why velocity decoders outperforms position decoder in general
\end{itemize}
