We have successsfully implemented GRU and other state-of-the-art deep learing techniques to reach higher decoding performances than previous studies.
All three kinds of kinematic variables, position, velocity and acceleration were improved with their $R^2$.
Compared to \cite{makin2018}, we advanced the decoding accuracy of acceleration the most,
and the x-axis results were boosted more than the y-axis is all three types of kinematics.
\\

Our research offered a novel way of neural information visualization.
With the aid of temporal attention module,
it is easier for human to gain insight into how does RNN learn from the input neural signals,
along with the fingertip trajectories, which are the output.
Our attention map showed an obvious pattern which is strongly related to the fingertip's movement,
which no other BCI researches have attained before.
\\

The interpretations our attention maps can provide are
\begin{itemize}
    \item reasons for choosing a specific number as the length of spiking history 
    \item bidirectional RNN surpasses one-way RNN in neural decoding
    \item splitting position, velocity and acceleration into 3 individual decoders
    \item velocity decoders outperforms position decoder in general
\end{itemize}

Our discovery in how deep learning learns from neural signal features also corresponds to the biological theories regarding the role of spiking signal in motor control,
Forging new bond between neuroscience and and neural networks.
