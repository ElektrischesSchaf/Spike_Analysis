
Neural spike decoding provides remarkable precision in BCIs.
In this research,
we developed a neural signal decoder to predict the hand movements of nonhuman primates with high prediction accuracy,
and the results surpassed the state-of-the-art methods.
The decoding performances were provided in three types of kinematic variables (position, velocity, accelerateion) and in both x and y-axis have achieved higher precisions than previous studies.
Using bidirectional recurrent neural networks (BRNNs) and self-attention mechanism,
not only can we reach higher level of performance but also provide a insightful visualization tool.
Our attention maps is capable of aidding researchers to design or evaluate the RNNs,
providing new aspects about the neural signals.
When it comes to determing the tap sizes for neural decoder,
arbitrarity and empirical methods are used by current researches.
We provided an analytical measure to visualize and quantify the optimal tap sizes for velocity and acceleration decoders.
Our attention patterns are also match by some theories in neuroscience,
and it gives the reason to explain why position decoders require longer input data.
