Neural spike decoding 

Developing a neural signal decoder to predict the hand movement of nonhuman primates with high prediction accuracy,
with the decoding results surpassed state-of-the-art methods.
The decoding performances were provided in three types of kinematic variables (position, velocity, accelerateion) and in both x and y-axis have achieved higher precisions than previous studies, 
which were based on the same nonhuman dataset.
Using bidirectional recurrent neural networks (BRNNs) and self-attention mechanism,
not only can we reach higher level of performance but also provide a insightful visualization tool.
Our attention maps is capable of aidding researchers to design or evaluate the RNNs, providing new aspects of the neural signals.
When it comes to determing the tap sizes for neural decoder,
arbitrarity and empirical methods are used by most researchers now.
We provided an analytical measure to visualize and quantify the optimal tap sizes for velocity and acceleration decoders.
Our attention patterns are also match by one theory in neuroscience,
and it gives the reason for position decoder requiring longer input data.
