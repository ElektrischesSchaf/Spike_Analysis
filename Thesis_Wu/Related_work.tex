Kalman filter was dominant in this field before the appearance of artificial neural networks.
Most of the experiments conducted in the traditional BCI reseaches were either center-out reaching task or the computer cursor manipulation with manipulandum.
The decoders usually predicted one or two kinematic variables, 
and only a few of them are able to predict and evaluate three variables: position, velocity and acceleration.


\todo[ author=Wu, color=blue!20]{新增Kalman filter的優缺點}
The strength of Kalman filter is that it only needs a few amount of training data \citep{wu2003neural},
applicable in real-time estimation \citep{wu2003neural},
and it is successfully applied in both open loop and close-loop BCIs \citep{gupta2009offline}.
The disadvantages, however, 
is that Kalman filter has assumed Gaussian distribution for neural activity and the decoded variables \citep{glaser2020machine, ahmadi2019decoding, todorova2017neural}.
Moreover, Kalman filter considers linear functions in the hidden state at time $t$ and its previous hidden state at time $t-1$ \citep{glaser2020machine}.
But neural spike signals are non-linear and non-Gaussian \citep{macke2011empirical, holleman2008micro},
and the Gaussian-distributed spike counts and linear dynamics assumptions are sometimes problematic \citep{makin2018}.

\cite{glaser2020machine} has analyzed the performance of modern methods and traditional methods in BCI motor decoding.
They take long short-term memory (LSTM) and ensemble models as the representative of modern methods, 
and they outperformed the traditional ones significantly such as Wiener filter, 
Wiener cascade and Kalman filter.
Various neural datasets they have used to evaluate the models,
some models such as naive Bayesian decoder, also a traditional method, can only perform well in the position prediction in the hippocampus dataset.
LSTM and other feedforward neural networks' performances are almost the same. 
They endorsed and provided detailed experimental result about the fact that the modern deep leaning methods can generally exceed the traditional methods.
When the amount of traing data is decreased, only Kalman filter can be comparable to modern methods wheras the performance of Wiener filter drops drastically.
Limited neural data also affect more on traditional methods than modern ones.
When decoding with only 10 neurons in their experiments, modern methods outperform traditional methods significantly.

\section{Advantages of deep learning in BCIs}

% \todo[inline, author=Wu, color=blue!20]{如 Section \ref{sec:restrictions_of_traditional_invasive_BCI} 所述，deep learning decoder雖然更複雜，在減少training data與 recorded neurons 的情況下仍有較好的表現 }
Deep learning models provided remarkable improvement to neural decoding \citep{hosman2019bci}.
Altought with its greater complexity, deep learning decoders still performs better with shorter training data and fewer recorded neurons \citep{glaser2020machine}.
In the early study of BCIs, it was the linear Kalman filter decoders which has been widely applied to intracortical brain computer interfaces \citep{jarosiewicz2015virtual, pandarinath2017high,nuyujukian2018cortical}.
With the development of non-linear decoders and deep learning methods, 
more promising performances have been reached both in work in humans and non-human primates \citep{shanechi2017rapid, sussillo2016making, milstein2017multiscale, pandarinath2018inferring}.
Among all the deep learning methods, 
the RNN model is the tpye which is widely used and also outperforms Kalman filter \citep{sussillo2016making}.


The first idea of recurrent neural networks (RNN), which is called the Hopfield networks, was introduced in 1980s \citep{hopfield1982neural}, 
and the following different versions of RNN have been developed afterwards \citep{elman1990finding, jordan1997serial}.
But the these RNN models face the same gradient vanishing problem, and the two effective approaches to solve this problem is either clip and scale the gradient if the norm is too large, 
or create a better RNN model \citep{alom2018history}.

% \end{multicols}

% \begin{multicols}{2}
Therefore, \cite{hochreiter1997long} created a well-known model LSTM, solved the gradient vanishing problem of the original RNNs. LSTM quipped with three gates to learn how to forget or keep the memory from previous states. 
Followed by the work of \cite{chung2014empirical}, a new model named Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) which is similar to LSTM, was introduced. 
It is has gained popularity in the community due to its efficiency in training and the high performance sometimes even outdoes the LSTM. 

Nowadays, RNNs come with various architectures have been widely used in BCI researches \citep{ahmadi2020impact}.
\cite{tuleuov2019deep} even developed a hybrid deep leaning model which combined RNN with convolution neural network (CNN). 
These preceding works manifest the uprising potentials of RNNs in computational neural science.

Deep learning methods have been criticized as black boxes for a long time \citep{brown2018recurrent, hudson2018compositional}. 
By pointing out the parts where the model cosider informative allows humans to have more insights into the decision factors inside deep learning.
\cite{tseng2019decoding} intended to show in internal dynamics of the LSTM when decoding the movement of monkeys, 
but by just showing the value of the gates in each LSTM timesteps (Section~\ref{sec:internal_dynamics_of_network}) simply does not informative enough.

In the field of natural language processing, researchers have successfully hightlighted the specific texts among a sentence or a paragraph, 
which are the most relevant to the translation or classification tasks.
Pointing out the dominant key words contribute the most in their deep learning result makes it easier for human to evaluate the strength of the model.


\cite{nakagome2020empirical} evaluated the effect of different tap sizes empirically,
from small tap sizes (1, 2, and 5) to large ones (30, 40, 50).
In general, performances of all methods increased as the tap size increased.
For Unscented Kalman filter (UKF) the best tap size is 5 and for linear decoders the optimal tap size is 20-30.
Neural network decoders such as LSTM or GRU required even more taps as far as 50.
Although their result could be the baseline for both traditional and modern neural decoders,
it lacks an interpretable proof and the explanation of these phenomena.

In the traning phase, decoder will be provided the actual target sequence wheras in testing phase,

But when it comes to processing long time-sequence data, inevitably will the models facing the problem called long-term dependencies. 
This is primarily due to the fact that the longer the sequence length, 
the higher the possibility of gradient vanishing in RNNs \citep{chandar2019towards}. 
Especially in neural machine translation researches,
where long input sentences are apt to cause translation errors.
Thus a novel architecture for neural machine translation was invented,
named \textit{alignment model}
It was proposed in \cite{bahdanau2014neural},
which they put additional weights on the original outputs of an RNN. 
The alighment model scores how well the inputs around one position matches the output on another position, 
such that model is capable of focusing on the information relevant part of the input sentence.


Apart form alignment model, \textit{self-attention} of \textit{intra-attention} works quite differently.
The alighment model calculate the relationship between source and target sequences, 
while self-attention only consider relationship beteen the elements from the same input sequence.
\cite{cheng2016long} proposed the \textit{intra-attention} mechanism first,
and followed by the work of \cite{parikh2016decomposable,paulus2017deep}.
Then \cite{vaswani2017attention} adapted similar idea but discarded the RNN architecture,
creating the \textit{Transformer} model with the advantage of parallelization.

Table~\ref{tab:rnn_attention_equations_study} listed the equations of different kinds of attention modules for RNNs.
It showes how the sequential attention evolved from sequence to sequence models \citep{bahdanau2014neural, luong2015effective}
into the ones for classification and sentiment analysis, 
by calculating the correlation from every two hidden states in the same sequence  \citep{cheng2016long, yang2016hierarchical}.
However, this procedure costs considerably amount of computational time, 
thus a simplified version known as local attention was introduced \citep{luong2015effective}.
Followed by the works of \cite{wang2016attention, tao2018get, ma2017dipole, hu2018novel,cui2019hierarchical}, 
all of them implemented similar local attention techniques.

Unlike the attention methods used in image processing as shown Table~\ref{tab:image_attention_equations_study}, 
attention in RNNs typically use hyperbolic tangent and softmax as activations, 
and the dimension to be annotated remains constant during the calcuation, 
wheras \cite{hu2018squeeze, woo2018cbam} used rectified linear unit (ReLU) and sigmoid instead, also reduce and then expand the dimension which the attention annotates.

Similar attention techniques can be seen in sentiment classification research \citep{wang2016attention}, 
where they appended the attention mechanism right after the LSTM outputs and named it attention-based LSTM.
They also visualized the aspect in each sentence, e.g., the term \textit{service} or \textit{food} by showing the weights in attention vectors.
They can tell the important words from the whole sentence.

Several different means of attention mechanism were also implemented in BCI field,
but attention modules are now only be implemented in non-invasive BCIs.
\cite{li2020multi} adopted the famous Squeeze-and-Excitation attention module \citep{hu2018squeeze},
and visualized the importance of input EEG signals with respect to time.
The intervals with high attention score correspond to the moment when subjects received the external stimuli, 
making the EEG signal at that time prominent to the classifications.

that is whether they were paying attention during the experiments or not.

\cite{zhang2020motor} used both CNN and RNN to extract the spatio and temporal dynamics in EEG signal processing,
and its mechanism named \textit{Convolutional Recurrent Attention Model}.
The self-attention module was placed in the last layer right before the classification layer,
after the raw signal was first processed by CNN and then RNN.
The proposed method, convolutional recurrent attention model outperformed other state-of-the-art methods in EEG classification tasks.
They also used the temporal attention module to understand how RNN learns from the EEG features.
Their results shown that the RNN outputs which are later in time have higher attention values,
indicating more contributions they provided to the classifications.
\citep{hu2018novel} adopted the hybrid CNN-RNN architecture.
They fed the sEMG signal into CNN layer first, then the RNN layer.
Immediately followed by the the attention module,
then after fully connected layers is the final classification results.
