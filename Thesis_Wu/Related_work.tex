Deep learning models provided remarkable improvement to neural decoding \citep{hosman2019bci}.
In the early study of BCIs, it was the linear Kalman filter decoders which has been widely applied to intracortical brain computer interfaces \citep{jarosiewicz2015virtual, pandarinath2017high,nuyujukian2018cortical}.
With to the development of non-linear decoders and deep learning methods, more promising performances have been reached both in work in humans and non-human primates \citep{shanechi2017rapid, sussillo2016making, milstein2017multiscale, pandarinath2018inferring}.
Among all the deep learning methods, the RNN model is the tpye which is widely used and also outperforms Kalman filter \citep{sussillo2016making}.

The first idea of recurrent neural networks (RNN), which is called the Hopfield networks, was introduced in 1980s \citep{hopfield1982neural}, 
and the following different versions of RNN have been developed afterwards \citep{elman1990finding, jordan1997serial}, as showed in Figure~\ref{fig:elman_and_jordan}. 
But the these RNN models face the same gradient vanishing problem, and the two effective approaches to solve this problem is either clip and scale the gradient if the norm is too large, 
or create a better RNN model \citep{alom2018history}.

But when it comes to processing long time-sequence data, inevitably will the models facing the problem called long-term dependencies. 
This is primarily due to the fact that the longer the sequence lenght, 
the higher the possibility of gradient vanishing might occur in RNNs \citep{chandar2019towards}. 
Especially in neural machine translation researches, where long input sentences are apt to cause translation errors.
Thus a novel architecture for neural machine translation customed to machine translation was invented, named \textit{alignment model}
It was proposed in \cite{bahdanau2014neural}, which they put an additional weights on the original outputs of an RNN. 
The alighment model scores how well the inputs around one position matches the output on another position, 
such that model is capable of focusing on the information relevant part of the input sentence, 
and leave a major positive impact on the ability of the neural machine translation system. 


Apart form alignment model, \textit{self-attention} of \textit{intra-attention} works quiet differently.
The alighment model calculate the relationship between source and target sequences, 
while self-attention only consider relationship beteen the elements from the same input sequence.
It was \cite{cheng2016long} which first proposed the \textit{intra-attention} mechanism, and followed by the work of \cite{parikh2016decomposable,paulus2017deep}.
Then \cite{vaswani2017attention} adapted similar idea but discarded the RNN architecture, making its \textit{Transformer} model has the advantage of parallelization.

\begin{multicols}{2}
Similar attention techniques can be seen in sentiment classification research \citep{wang2016attention}, 
where they appended the attention mechanism right after the LSTM outputs and named it attention-based LSTM.
They also visualized the aspect in each sentence, e.g., the term "service" or "food" by showing the weights in attention vectors.
They can tell the important words from the whole sentence.

Several different means of attention mechanism were also implemented in BCI field.
\cite{zhang2020motor} used both CNN and RNN to extract the spatio and temporal dynamics in EEG signal processing,
and its mechanism named \textit{Convolutional Recurrent Attention Model}.
The self-attention module was placed in the last layer right before the classification layer, after the raw signal was first processed by CNN and then RNN.
The proposed method, convolutional recurrent attention model outperformed other state-of-the-art methods in EEG classification tasks.
\cite{hu2018novel} also constructed the hybrid CNN-RNN architecture.
They feed the sEMG signal into CNN layer first, then the RNN layer. 
Immediately followed by the the attention module, then after fully connected layers, is the final classification to show which geture the subject was executing.

\end{multicols}
