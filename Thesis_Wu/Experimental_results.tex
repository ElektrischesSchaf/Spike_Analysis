\subsection{Position decoder}
\begin{multicols}{2}
Most sessions benefit from the layer normalization and attention decoder, 
but there are sessions turned out to have particularly poor performances.
Take session indy\_20161027\_03 for example, 
the x and y-axis position prediction $R^2$ in \cite{makin2018} is (0.41, 0.72),
and in our bidirectional LN-GRU with attention module the performance is (0.51, 0.55).

The corresponding attention map is showed in Figure~\ref{fig:indy_20161027_03_corresponding_attention_map},
and we can tell that our decoder with attention failed when it has lose the focus a the time bins.
In other words, the attention scores are distributed rather evenly among all the past time bins,
whereas in the samples of good prediction accuracy, 
the attention scores are more concentrated on some specific past time bins.
our attention should able to distinguish if the fingertip of the subjects is moving or stationary.
This not an easy task since neurons still fire when the subjects hold their movement.

\end{multicols}
