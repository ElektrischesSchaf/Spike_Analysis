Developing a state-of-the-art neural signal decoder to predict the hand movement of nonhuman primates with high prediction accuracy.

Using recurrent neural networks (RNNs) and self-attention mechanism, not only can we reach higher level of performance but also provide a insightful visualization tool, 
aidding researchers to design or evaluate the RNNs and provide new aspects of the neural signals.

The decoding performances in three types of kinematic variables (position, velocity, accelerateion) in both x- and y-directions have achieved higher precisions than previous studies, based on the same nonhuman dataset.

\textit{Brain-machine Interface, Spike Detection, Recurrent Neural Network, Attention Machenism}
